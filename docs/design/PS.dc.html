REQUEST FOR ETLS FACILITY: BANJUL BAKERY SUPPLIES

This Ministry is in receipt of an application for the clearance of goods (wheat flour) from the above-mentioned company.

Please be informed that BANJUL BAKERY SUPPLIES is importing 2,000 bags of wheat flour into The Gambia from Kossi Milling SARL of Togo under the approved list of products benefitting from the ECOWAS Trade Liberalization Scheme. Kossi Milling SARL is an ETLS-certified company with approval number 768-0057 and product approval number 768-0057-01-25.

We hereby write to your good office to kindly exempt these products from paying duties as specified in the ETLS protocol.

Attached herewith are copies of the supporting documents, i.e. Certificate of Origin (No. 20418), for your kind consideration.

Sincerely,

Permanent Secretary
